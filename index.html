Loom Logic

Work up from small pieces of cloth to the rule that wove it.

How it works
  1. The top row is where the cloth starts. Every row below it is made from the row directly above.
  2. Each cell looks at the three cells above it: up-left, straight up, and up-right. Anything past the left or right edge counts as empty.
  3. There are only 8 possible three-cell patterns. The rule card says what each one makes: a filled cell or an empty cell. One card is used for the whole cloth.
  4. The card is hidden. You get clue marks instead. A solid diamond means the cell is filled. A hollow diamond means the cell is empty.
  5. Set the rule card, and any top-row cells with a yellow dashed outline, until the whole cloth is woven and every clue matches.

A good way in: find a clue whose three cells above it are already known. That clue tells you one rule entry for certain. Grey dotted cells are not known yet.

Switch to Mark to pencil in what you have worked out about a cell: a dot for filled, a ring for empty. Marks are only notes. They never change the cloth.

No timer, no sound, no score to lose. Every move can be undone. Keyboard: arrow keys move around the cloth, 1 to 8 switch rule entries, M switches Look and Mark, Z undoes, Y redoes.
